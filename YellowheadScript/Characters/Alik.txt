Characterization


Success is the only thing I understand. Atlas wants to be on top. He can't stand the cage he is in, and will do anything to rise above it.
Atlas thinks he is smarter and better than the people around him, this causes him to ignore their input and continue his quest for power.

Atlas goes to the witch for power, but the power is corrupting him and he ends up killing his brother.
