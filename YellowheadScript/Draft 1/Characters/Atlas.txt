Early on in the story Atlas begins burning his soul, over time this changes his appearance. 
His body glows faintly, and his body fluids are fire when he is powered up. His skin gets progressively 
warmer until he is too hot to touch. The area around his eyes is perpetually burnt. The pain of the heat
gets to him more and more, and you see him submerging himself in cold water often.

A major plot throughout Atlas's story is his rise through the ranks of the dark lord.
