Alik looked out over his fathers city. The white stones gleamed under the morning sun.
He could see the procession, winding through the switchbacks up the city terraces.
Behind him, a woman approached and stood respectfully a few feet away.
"Everything is ready for you, my lord."
Alik turned to look at her.
"So soon? The witch won't arrive until mid-day"
"If it displeases you, I'll have the men return..."
"No no, well done Pheobe" Alik said laughingly and waved his hand dismissivly 
"I imagine the other lords will still be scrambling to arrange their parties by the time she arrives"
"We were the first" Pheobe said proudly
Alik smiled. "I should go see the men" he said stepping down from the ledge
He was a youth of 19, tall, and well built. 
He had shaggy blonde hair and blue eyes.
He walked as though the ground itself belonged to him and here, in this city, that wasn't entirely untrue.
Pheobe was a couple years his senior, tall and a bit thin. She had brown eyes under her spectacles, and kept her light brown hair pulled back in a tight bun.
"Wait" she said
"Your father is at the assembly"
Alik paused
"Your armorer is waiting for you in your chambers"
